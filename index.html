Grammar: Am, Is and Are

Use am with I.

Example: I am a student.

Use is with he, she, it and one person's name.

Example: She is happy. / Rahim is kind.

Use are with you, we, they and plural nouns.

Example: They are friends. / You are welcome.

Remember: I am · He/She/It is · You/We/They are
